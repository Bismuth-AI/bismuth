# Third-party software and model notices

This document identifies the direct third-party components declared by Bismuth. The
version column records the constraint in `pyproject.toml`; the exact resolved version and
the complete transitive dependency inventory are generated by the `licences` CI job for
each commit and uploaded as the `third-party-licenses` artifact.

Bismuth does not vendor these packages. They are installed from their normal Python
distribution channels and remain subject to their own licenses. This notice is provided
for attribution and does not change those licenses.

## AI models and external model services

Bismuth contains no model weights, training datasets, fine-tuning adapters, or generated
weights. It uses an existing model unchanged through a provider-neutral boundary. Model
selection is made by the operator, and each model remains subject to its own license or
service terms.

The open-weight reference model used for full-pipeline verification is:

| Model | Use type | License | Official source | Distribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 | Existing external model without additional training | Apache-2.0 | [Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8) | Publicly downloadable model weights |

Anthropic and OpenAI are optional hosted providers. Bismuth does not redistribute their
models or grant rights to them. Operators who select a hosted provider must comply with
that provider's applicable service terms. A hosted provider is not required: an
OpenAI-compatible endpoint operated locally or on an independently controlled server is
supported.
